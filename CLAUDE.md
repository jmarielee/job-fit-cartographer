# Routing

This file routes. It holds no map content.

**To read the map of Job Fit Scanner:** open `map/catalog.md`. Find your row.
Open that one card. Stop. Do not load `map/cards/` wholesale.

**Before trusting a row:** `cartographer/reference/collisions.md`. Five words
in this territory carry more than one meaning.

**To use the cartographer on a different body of work:** `cartographer/`.
That folder travels on its own and has its own front door —
`cartographer/README.md`. Start there.

**To check the map against its source:** `node verify.js` from the repo root.

**Everything else is evidence, not map.** `walks/`, `portability/` and
`EVIDENCE.md` are the record of how this was tested. None of it is required
to walk the map.
